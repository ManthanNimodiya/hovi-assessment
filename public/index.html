Rent a car with your digital driving licence

A prototype with three parties: a licence authority issues a digital driving licence to the customer's wallet (OpenID4VCI). A car rental company asks the wallet for only the licence details it needs (OpenID4VP), checks them, and decides whether the customer may drive the chosen vehicle for the whole rental period. All data is synthetic.

1. Licence office

Issue a driving licence to a wallet, and revoke it later.

3. Wallet

Use the built-in demo wallet, or any EUDI-compatible wallet app that supports SD-JWT VC.

Open demo wallet

Try the whole flow in 2 minutes

  1. Licence office: issue a licence (the form is pre-filled with a synthetic person) and click Open in demo wallet.
  2. Demo wallet: enter the PIN shown by the office and receive the licence.
  3. Car rental: pick a vehicle and dates, then click Open in demo wallet and share.
  4. See the decision with every check explained. Then revoke the licence in the office and try again: it's rejected.

Also try: a motorcycle with a car-only licence, a licence that expires during the rental, a driver under 21 for the Tesla, or a licence from the unofficial (untrusted) office.
